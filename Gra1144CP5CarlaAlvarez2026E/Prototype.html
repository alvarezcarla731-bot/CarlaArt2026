01 / The Artist

More Than
A Voice.

RAYE is a British singer and songwriter known for her powerful vocals, honest storytelling, and genre-defying artistry.

Blending jazz, soul, R&B, and contemporary pop, she has created a distinctive sound that celebrates artistic independence and vulnerability.

Her music is a journey through emotion, self-discovery, heartbreak, and empowerment.
